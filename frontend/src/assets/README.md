## Meadow prompt

Create a photorealistic editorial photograph for the Job Ready career website landing-page hero. A beautiful rolling green meadow, fine blades of grass in foreground, a narrow gently curving walking path leading toward the horizon, a solitary graceful tree off-center, soft early morning sunlight and pale sky. Serene, hopeful, natural, sophisticated editorial landscape photography, muted rich forest greens and sage, subtle film grain, real textures. Landscape 3:2 composition, no people, no buildings, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermark. The image will be shown beside a large serif headline about an honest next step in your career.

## Workspace prompt

Use case photorealistic-natural. Create an editorial photograph for Job Ready, a thoughtful honest career preparation website. A close-up sunlit oak desk beside a window overlooking softly blurred green foliage. On desk an open cream notebook with blank pages, a fine dark green pen, closed matte sage green notebook and a small ceramic cup of coffee; just the edge of a silver laptop visible. Carefully composed natural still life, understated real workspace, beautiful diagonal morning shadows, forest greens and neutral cream. Landscape 3:2, elevated editorial photography with subtle film grain, no people, no legible words, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## CV review prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an editorial photograph for Job Ready, a website helping people take one step ahead in their job search. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A young adult South Asian woman at a modest bright home workspace thoughtfully reviewing a printed CV beside her open laptop, pen resting in hand and a notebook nearby. Candid three-quarter side view, quietly confident focused expression, genuine preparation rather than celebration. Subject and meaningful objects centered so the image can be cropped wide or square. Soft natural window light, forest green shirt, pale cool neutral walls, natural wood table, realistic skin and hands, refined editorial photography, subtle grain. Make the scene clearly about preparing a job application. Paper contains subtle unreadable typographic lines, laptop screen turned away. No readable text, no logos, no watermark, no floating interface elements, no handshake, no staged thumbs-up.

## Practice prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Editorial photograph for Job Ready job-search preparation website, companion to a natural window-lit portrait of a woman reviewing a CV. Landscape 3:2. Two adult friends practicing and learning together at a library work table: a Black woman in a muted sage shirt and a South Asian man in an off-white overshirt, both about late twenties, focused approachable expressions, discussing an open laptop and making notes in a notebook. Candid eye-level medium shot, faces and working hands grouped centrally for square crop. Modest real setting, blurred bookshelves, soft daylight, cool pale neutral walls, forest green accents, natural wood, refined editorial photography with realistic texture. They are actively working through a problem, not posing for camera. No readable text, no logos, no watermark, no handshakes, no hiring celebration, no floating UI.
