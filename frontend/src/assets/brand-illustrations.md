## Focused CV prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create an original brand illustration for Job Ready in tactile paper-sculpture and matte clay miniature style, NOT a photograph of people, NOT an office or desk scene. Square 1:1 composition with generous margins so all objects remain visible. Concept: moving from scattered generic applications to one focused role-specific CV. At lower left a small loose fan of pale grey application sheets with abstract embossed lines, leading into three equal ascending forest-green steps across the scene; at the top step one upright beautiful ivory CV sheet with neatly aligned short green relief lines, facing a small matching upright role card. A clear physical progression, calm and optimistic, no arrows. On the front central face of the staircase place a prominent dark forest green rounded square badge carrying EXACTLY the white equal-segment staircase logo from the supplied reference image, no arrowhead. Beneath that badge neatly print the exact brand name 'Job Ready' in restrained dark-green sans-serif lettering on an ivory plaque. Use the reference only as the exact logo, not as the whole composition. Forest green #1b6b45, pale mint, cool ivory background #f6f7f6, fine paper edges, soft sculptural shadows, sophisticated editorial art direction, front three-quarter isometric view, clean uncluttered composition. All CV content is abstract lines, no invented names or facts, no other readable text, no people, no laptops, no plants, no trophies, no logos except Job Ready.
