How It Works

Upload the CV. Paste the job.

Upload

A text PDF or DOCX up to 5 MB. Scans are rejected. The original file is never stored—parsed instantly into structured data.

Paste

The role title and job description (up to 20,000 characters). Required and preferred skills are extracted cleanly in seconds.

Required skills are marked on the CV. The fit score is calculated from those skills and the years already on the profile.
