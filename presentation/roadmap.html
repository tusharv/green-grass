An Actionable Roadmap Over an Empty Rejection.

Traditional applications end in silence or generic automated rejections. Green Grass turns a gap into a clear path forward:

Prioritization

Major vs. Minor Gaps

Missing required skills are flagged as Major Gaps; missing preferred skills as Minor Gaps. Candidates immediately see the exact blockers that disqualify their candidacy.

Understanding

Why It Matters

Every identified gap is paired with a clear explanation of why this competency is crucial to this role's engineering duties, helping candidates build real domain context.

Proof Project

Hands-on Projects

The practice report recommends a concrete, self-contained project to build and deploy so candidates return with demonstrable proof of capability.
