The Problem

The Hiring Dilemma: Generic Spam & AI Hallucinations.

Job seekers face a broken hiring pipeline, and existing automated tools only make it worse:

Volume Trap

The Spray-and-Pray Trap

Sending 200 generic CVs into applicant tracking systems (ATS) yields near-zero callbacks, overwhelming recruiters with noise and exhausting candidates.

AI Pitfall

AI Builders That Lie

Generative resume rewriters hallucinate skills, inflate metrics, and invent responsibilities to match keywords—setting candidates up to fail technical interviews.

Black Box

Rejection Without Signal

Candidates get generic automated rejection emails with zero feedback. They never learn what skills were missing or what they need to build to get hired.
