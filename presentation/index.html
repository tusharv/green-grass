What is Green Grass

Your CV is either ready to send, or you get what to practice.

Green Grass is an honest CV evaluation platform. It verifies your real experience against one specific job posting in code—polishing your wording if you clear the bar, or providing a targeted practice plan if you have gaps. It never invents experience.
