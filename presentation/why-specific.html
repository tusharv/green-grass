Why a Job-Specific CV is Non-Negotiable.

Recruiters spend an average of 6 to 7 seconds on an initial scan. Specific relevance always beats generic volume:

7-Second Scan

High Signal in 7 Seconds

Hiring teams scan for proof of 3–5 role-critical competencies. A job-specific CV brings relevant evidence to the top instead of burying it under unrelated bullet points.

Evidence

Context Over Buzzwords

Engineering leaders look for evidence of depth—how tools were used in production environments—rather than an alphabet soup of keywords stuffed into a skills header.

Integrity

Honest Alignment

True tailoring re-contextualizes your genuine accomplishments into the vocabulary of the hiring team, preserving employers, dates, and metrics with complete fidelity.
